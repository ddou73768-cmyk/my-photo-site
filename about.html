BEHIND THE LENS

About me.

MOMENT

摄影师 · 中国

I photograph people, places, and the quiet details of everyday life. Through light and shadow, I look for moments that feel honest — a passing expression, an unfamiliar street, or something ordinary seen anew.

MOMENT is a collection of those observations. My work moves between portraiture, landscapes, and daily life, with a simple intention: to pause, look closer, and remember.
